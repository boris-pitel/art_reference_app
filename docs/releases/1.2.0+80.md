# Painter Reference 1.2.0+80

Released 2026-08-27.

## Features and improvements

- You can now report a message, an image someone sent you, or the person
  themselves. Long press anything they sent, or use the menu at the top of the
  conversation. Reporting offers to block them at the same time.

## Bugs fixed

- Nothing in this release. See 1.2.0+78 for the thumbnail fix.

## Behind the scenes

- The tables holding images and categories are now closed to anyone who is not
  signed in as their owner. They were readable with the key that ships in the
  app, which is public by design.
