# Painter Reference 1.2.0+81

Released 2026-08-27.

## Features and improvements

- Sign in with Apple, alongside Google and email. Apple requires this wherever
  a third-party sign-in is offered, because it lets you keep your email
  address private.

## Bugs fixed

- Nothing in this release.

## Worth knowing

- Choosing "Hide My Email" when signing in with Apple gives you a relay
  address, which is a different address from your usual one. Since a library
  belongs to an email address, that means a new, empty library. Share your
  real address to land on the one you already have.

## Platforms

- Windows only. Web, Android and iOS remain on 1.2.0+80.
